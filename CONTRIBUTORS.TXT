Contributors
============

Course
======

The course content was developed by Wesam Salem, Ben Pierce, and Piotr
Mitros for ELED 3261 at the University of Memphis, drawing on the Ambitious
Science Teaching framework. Wesam Salem is the course instructor; Ben Pierce
and Piotr Mitros did most of the authoring and revision, working with
Anthropic's Claude models, which drafted much of the material under their
direction and review.

Platform
========

This module is built with lo-blocks, part of the Learning Observer project,
originally conceived of and created by Piotr Mitros. See the lo-blocks
repository (https://github.com/olxhub/lo-blocks/) for full platform
contributor history.
